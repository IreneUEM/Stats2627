What matters most

Three things that decide your year

The final exam is 35% and you must score at least 5.0 in it. Everything else you do all term only counts once you clear that bar.
The Flipped Classroom is worth 25%, more than the midterm. To be allowed to present, your team must have submitted all three progress uploads.
The JiTT assignments are worth 5%, but you get the points just for doing them. Keep in mind that if you stop doing them in October, that 5% cannot be recovered.

The project, step by step

The three uploads go to the Virtual Campus and do not change what we do in class: they are how I see your real progress and can steer you in time.

Upload 1Fri 16 Oct

You pick a topic and a dataset and state your research question, with the response variable and the candidate factors.

Upload 2Fri 13 Nov

Data in hand: descriptive analysis, a check that the design holds up, and a log of any deviations.

Upload 3Fri 4 Dec

The full analysis using the methods covered in class, results interpreted and a draft of your conclusions.

Flipped Classroom11, 14 & 18 Dec

You teach: 60 minutes on your topic and experiment, plus questions from the class.

What you need to know

The rules that cost the most marks every year.

Attendance 50% floor

Attendance is taken every session. Below 50% you may sit the ordinary exam but it will not be graded. Verified attendance via Teams counts when your absence is justified.

To pass two conditions

You need a weighted average of 5.0 and also 5.0 in the final exam. Below 5.0 in the final, nothing else can be averaged in.

JiTT quizzes 18:00 before class day

Each pre-class quiz closes at 18:00 CET on the day before the class. Wrong answers are not penalised: what counts is doing them, on time.

Submissions 23:59

All written work is due at 23:59 on the stated date, on the Virtual Campus. Late work is penalised; the absolute cut-off is the day before the ordinary exam.

Your team 4-5 people

Teams of 4 to 5. The three progress uploads are compulsory: miss one and your team cannot present, losing the whole 25%. Your teaching slot is 60 minutes plus questions.

Plagiarism & AI read this

Plagiarism means losing the assessment period and it goes on your record. AI use must be authorised for each activity and declared: you remain the author.
